# Regulatory Reference — Process Safety Management (OSHA 1910.119)

**Document ID:** REG-PSM
**Doc type:** regulatory
**Authority:** OSHA
**Reference:** 29 CFR 1910.119
**Effective date:** 2023-01-01
**Applies to:** Riverside Plant / Cooling Water Unit (covered process)

Selected requirements relevant to the cooling-water pumps and procedures.

| Req ID | Requirement | Frequency | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| PSM-PSI | Process Safety Information (equipment specs, materials) must be complete and current. | continuous | P-101, P-103, HX-3 |
| PSM-MOC | **Management of Change**: any change to process, procedure, or equipment must have a documented MOC (hazard review, approval, records updated) **before** implementation. | per change | all procedures incl. SOP-12 |
| PSM-MI | Mechanical Integrity: written procedures, inspections, and maintenance records maintained for covered equipment. | ongoing | P-101, P-103 |
| PSM-OP | Operating procedures must be current and reviewed periodically. | annual review | SOP-12 |

## Compliance-relevant facts present in the data
- SOP-12 was revised to **rev3** (effective 2024-11-04) changing the flange-torque value. **No MOC record exists** for this change.
- Equipment specifications conflict across documents (torque, lubricant), so PSI is **not current/consistent**.

> **DEMO NOTE:** This regulation establishes the requirements the Compliance agent checks. The deliberate **gap** is PSM-MOC — SOP-12 rev3 is a procedure change with no Management-of-Change record — and a secondary **conflict** under PSM-PSI because the equipment specs disagree across sources. Running *Assess* on P-101 / SOP-12 surfaces both, with citations to the requirement and the (missing) evidence. This is the Compliance beat.
