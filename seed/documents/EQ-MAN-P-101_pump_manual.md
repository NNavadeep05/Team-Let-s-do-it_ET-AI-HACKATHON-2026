**Plant / Unit:** Riverside Plant / Cooling Water Unit

## 1. Description
P-101 is a single-stage, end-suction centrifugal pump serving the cooling-water circuit. It draws from the cooling-water sump and discharges through isolation valve V-12 to heat exchanger HX-3. Pump discharge pressure is monitored by transmitter PT-204; bearing temperature by TT-209.

> **Note (manufacturer):** Discharge-flange bolts (M16, grade 8.8) shall be tightened to **95 Nm** in a star pattern across three passes. Over-torquing distorts the seal chamber and shortens mechanical-seal life.

## 3. Lubrication
Bearings are grease-lubricated. Use **ISO VG 68** mineral oil grade equivalent grease. Re-lubrication interval: 4000 operating hours.

## 4. Alignment
Shaft-to-driver alignment must be verified after any coupling work. Maximum permissible parallel offset 0.05 mm; angular 0.04 mm/100 mm. Misalignment is the leading cause of premature seal and bearing failure on this model.

## 5. Maintenance
Inspect mechanical seal and bearing vibration quarterly. Replace mechanical seal at first sign of weeping. Refer to site SOP-12 for the flange-bolt maintenance procedure.
