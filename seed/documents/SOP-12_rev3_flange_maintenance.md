## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines flange-bolt maintenance and re-torque for cooling-water pump discharge flanges following seal or coupling work.

## 2. Scope
Applies to DN80 discharge flanges with M16 grade 8.8 bolting on assets P-101 and P-103.

## 3. Procedure
1. Isolate the pump: close V-12, lock out, verify zero pressure at PT-204.
2. Relieve residual pressure and drain the casing.
3. Replace the mechanical seal cartridge per the manufacturer manual.
4. Re-fit the discharge flange. **Tighten the M16 flange bolts to 120 Nm** in a star pattern over three passes.
5. Verify shaft alignment (parallel ≤ 0.05 mm, angular ≤ 0.04 mm/100 mm).
6. Remove lockout, re-pressurize, and confirm discharge pressure at PT-204 within 5%.
