## Notes
Technicians have standardized on **140 Nm** for the discharge-flange bolts based on shop-floor practice ("they kept weeping at the book value"). Bearing re-lube has used **ISO VG 46** from general stock rather than the manual-specified grade. Vibration has trended upward across the last two quarters.

> **DEMO NOTE:** This log supplies the field torque value (**140 Nm**) and the off-spec lubricant (**ISO VG 46**). With the manual (95 Nm / ISO VG 68) already loaded, this creates the **two open contradictions** present at the start of the demo. Ingesting SOP-12 rev3 (120 Nm) live escalates the torque conflict to three-way. The elevated/trending vibration plus the repeat seal failures and un-verified alignment are the evidence that lets the RCA agent converge on **shaft misalignment**.
